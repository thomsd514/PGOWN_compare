# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe and respectful environment for everyone who participates in this project, regardless of background, identity, experience or role.

## Expected behaviour

Project participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- welcome questions and differing perspectives;
- provide feedback about the work rather than the person;
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them promptly;
- respect privacy, confidentiality and intellectual property;
- follow applicable Province of British Columbia policies and standards; and
- help maintain an environment free from harassment and discrimination.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- harassment, intimidation, discrimination or threats;
- insulting, demeaning or inflammatory comments;
- unwelcome sexual attention or sexualized content;
- deliberate misgendering or use of rejected names;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sustained disruption of project discussions or collaboration;
- retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith; and
- any other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions and other project spaces. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project or the Province of British Columbia in related public spaces.

## Reporting concerns

Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through an approved Government of British Columbia communication channel. Before publication, maintainers must replace this paragraph with a monitored team contact or documented internal reporting route.

For an immediate threat, contact the appropriate emergency service. For workplace harassment, discrimination, privacy, security or safety matters, use the applicable Province of British Columbia reporting and escalation process.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers will review reports as promptly and impartially as practical. Depending on the circumstances, responses may include:

1. a private clarification or request to stop;
2. a formal warning;
3. removal or rejection of content;
4. temporary restriction from participation;
5. permanent removal from the project; or
6. referral through an applicable Government of British Columbia process.

Maintainers will protect the privacy of reporters and affected participants to the extent reasonably possible and permitted by policy and law.

## Good-faith reports

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern or participates in a review in good faith is not acceptable. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves be addressed as a Code of Conduct violation.

## Attribution

This project-specific Code of Conduct is informed by common open-source community standards and is intended to operate alongside applicable Government of British Columbia workplace requirements and policies.
